A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Design is where curiosity becomes something you can see.

I'm Linta, a graphic designer interested in identity, typography, colour, campaigns and all the little details that make a visual feel intentional.

I like work that has personality. Something can be polished without becoming boring, playful without becoming messy, and simple without becoming forgettable.
